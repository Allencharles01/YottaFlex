| 1 | 2026-11-19 | **Device vs Logger split.** *Device* (`DEV-000001`) is a physical logger: 1,000 of them, reused across shipments, holding calibration data. *Logger* (`LOG-<shipment-id>-01`) is the assignment of one device to one shipment (one row per shipment, 1:1 with Shipment). A device may never have two assignments with overlapping time windows, except where an anomaly is deliberately injected. | The brief asks for 1,000 loggers but 5,000 shipments, and uses the shipment-scoped ID `LOG-<shipment-id>-01`. Splitting the physical device from its assignment resolves the contradiction and keeps both the brief's ID format and its volumes. |

| 32 | 2026-11-19 | **Trip model.** Road distance = great-circle distance between the party cities × `road_factor` (1.3), at `average_speed_kmh` (50). Planned trip = drive + `planned_dwell_min` per hub, rounded to the minute and clipped to `trip_duration_hours`. Actual = departure delay U[−20, 120] min (early departures allowed), each leg's planned drive × U[0.95, 1.15], dwell U[20, 120] min. Shipment times are whole minutes. Logger windows are `floor_grid(actual_departure − lead)` … `ceil_grid(actual_arrival + tail)`, with lead and tail U[10, 60] min. | Uses the reference coordinates, so durations are plausible for Indian routes (in-memory full-scale check: median ≈ 35 h, ≈ 2 % at the 72 h cap, ≈ 3 % at the 4 h floor for same-city trips; starter median 31.5 h). | 33 | 2026-11-19 | **Device scheduling.** Each device has one calibration record: `calibrated_on` = window start − U[0, 240] days, valid 365 days, so some devices expire during the window and are not used afterwards. | 34 | 2026-11-19 | **Lots.** Lots per product = max(`min_per_product`, ⌈shipments × mean lots per shipment / products / `shipments_per_lot`⌉).
